Ava Tran Jolly

M.S. Student at UC Santa Barbara

I graduated with a B.A. in Biology from the College of Creative Studies at UCSB in 2026. I'm broadly interested in phytochemistry and plant-insect coevolution, but my current research focuses on the ecology and evolution of tropane alkaloid content in Datura wrightii on Santa Cruz Island. Outside of research, I can usually be found hiking, tending to my 153 houseplants, reading poetry, or trying to teach myself piano for the millionth time.

About Me

I am a graduate student in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology at UC Santa Barbara, where I study questions related to plant ecology and evolution.

I am interested in understanding how interactions between plants and their environments shape variation in plant traits, including chemical defenses, and how this variation may influence evolutionary processes.

Outside of research, I enjoy learning about the natural world and exploring the connections between ecology and evolution.
